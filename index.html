About MP AutoPower

MP AutoPower is a new startup built on one idea: every urban customer in Madhya Pradesh should get the best possible deal on batteries and tyres, right in their own city — not just in the big metros.

We're launching directly into six cities — Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Ashoknagar, Lalitpur and Satna — so each regional office can quote local, competitive rates instead of one-size-fits-all metro pricing.

Every order is confirmed on a call or WhatsApp before a technician is dispatched, so you always know the exact brand, model and price before we arrive.

Frequently asked

Do you deliver and fit at home or office? +

Yes — every battery and tyre order includes doorstep delivery and fitting within our six office service areas.

How does the old battery/tyre exchange work? +

Hand over your old battery or tyre at the time of fitting and the exchange discount shown on the product card is deducted from your bill immediately.

Can I get a quote before deciding? +

Add items to your enquiry list and send it on WhatsApp, or request a callback — either way you get a firm price before anything is dispatched.

Do you serve commercial fleets and tractors? +

Yes, all six offices handle commercial vehicle and tractor batteries and tyres, including standing fleet accounts.

What if my city isn't one of the six offices? +

Call your nearest office — many nearby towns are covered on request even outside the core service area.
